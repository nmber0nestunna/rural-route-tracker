Rural Route Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Rural Route Tracker (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps rural mail carriers log their daily route numbers and estimate how their route compares with its evaluation. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your email, your password (stored securely by our backend), and the route numbers you enter.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Email address and passwordTo create your account, sign you in, and send sign-up and password-reset codesOur backend (Supabase). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
Route details from your evaluation forms (for example route number, route type, evaluated hours, box counts, daily averages, effective date)To calculate your averages and forecastsOn your device and in your account on our backend
Daily logs you enter (parcels, door trips, pickups, coverages, minutes and similar counts)To calculate your averages, forecasts and monthly summariesOn your device and in your account on our backend
App settings (what you track, rates, residents/business box split)To apply your choices on every device you sign in onOn your device and in your account on our backend
Evaluation PDF or image files you uploadTo read the numbers off your formsOnly on your device. The files are read on your phone and are not uploaded to us.
Reminder preferencesTo schedule your daily reminder and monthly summary noticeOnly on your device. Notifications are scheduled locally; we do not use push notification services.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The app can only open a photo or file that you choose to upload.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app’s features: storing your logs, syncing them between your devices, and showing your results.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as sign-up confirmation codes and password-reset codes.
  • To respond to you if you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database and account sign-in. Your account data is protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers account emails such as confirmation and password-reset codes, so it receives your email address and the message content.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Deleting your Rural Route Tracker account and data

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app, go to Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, your evaluation details and every day you have logged from our backend, and removes the app’s copy from that device.
  • Or email us at rural.route.tracker@gmail.com from your account’s email address and we will delete it for you.

Deleting removes your account, your evaluation details and all of your logged days. We don’t keep a copy afterward. Emails you sent us stay in our inbox unless you ask us to delete them too.

Deletion cannot be undone. You can export your logs as a CSV file from Settings → Your logs before deleting.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules on our database limit each account’s data to that account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The app is intended for adults who work as rural mail carriers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your information in the app at any time, export your logs, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or delete your data; contact us and we will help.

Not affiliated with USPS or the NRLCA

Rural Route Tracker is an independent app. It does not represent the United States Postal Service or any other government entity, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the United States Postal Service or the National Rural Letter Carriers’ Association (NRLCA). Results shown in the app are estimates for informational purposes only and are not a guarantee of any outcome.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app.
