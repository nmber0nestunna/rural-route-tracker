Help test Rural Route Tracker

An app for rural carriers to log daily counts and see whether the route will hold its Evaluated Hours. Android only for now. Takes about 2 minutes.

Use the same Google account as your phone’s Play Store in every step. If you’re asked to sign in, pick that account. Using a different one is the main reason the app doesn’t show up.

  1. Join the tester group

    Tap Join group on the page that opens. Other members can’t see your email.

    Open the tester group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. This only works after step 1.

    Open the test sign-up

  3. Install the app

    Install it from the Play Store. If it isn’t there yet, wait a few minutes after step 2 and try again.

    Open in Play Store
Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google requires that before the app can be released to everyone. Leaving it installed and logging a few days is the biggest help.

Create your own account in the app; don’t share one. It’s fine to start with made-up numbers for a day or two. Problems or ideas: tap Send feedback in Settings, or email rural.route.tracker@gmail.com.
